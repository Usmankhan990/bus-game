Privacy Policy – Bus Jam Party

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game Bus Jam Party ("the app"), developed by CodedHouse ("we") handles information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information such as your name, email address or phone number. The app does not require an account.

Your game progress (current level, coins, boosters and sound/vibration settings) is saved only on your device and is never sent to us.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, approximate location (based on IP address), device information, and how you interact with ads. This information is handled by Google according to its own privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time from the "Privacy settings" button on the home screen, when it is shown.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads / Privacy → Ads).

Children

The app is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data security

Data sent by the advertising SDK is encrypted in transit.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
